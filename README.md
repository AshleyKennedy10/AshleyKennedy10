## Hi, I'm Ashley 👋

My name is **Ashley Kennedy** and I am a Master of Data Science student at the University of Pittsburgh with a background in math and a strong interest in uncovering the stories hidden in data. I love working through complex problems and experimenting with different modeling approaches until the results make sense, both mathematically and intuitively.

I primarily work in Python and R and am drawn to projects that involve strategic predictive modeling, clustering, and key data visualizations.
